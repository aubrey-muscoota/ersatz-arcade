# Starspin: design one-pager

*Working title. Name, store listing and seller are not final.*

## The game in one line
A beam sweeps around the center of a galaxy. Tap when it hits the ring, and a star lands there. Hit every ring on time and you build a full spiral galaxy of 144 stars.

Each star sits 137.5° from the last (the "golden angle"). That spacing packs the stars into an even spiral, which is why the result looks so good.

## Rules
| Tap within | Shows | Points |
|---|---|---|
| ±4° | Perfect! | 3 × streak multiplier |
| ±10° | Great | 2 |
| ±18° | Close | 1 |
| further | Miss | 0, and you lose one of 3 lives |

- Every 5 perfects in a row adds 1× to the multiplier, up to 4×.
- **Supernovas:** about 1 ring in 13 glows gold. Hitting it is worth 5×.
- **Milestones** at 8, 21, 55, 89 and 144 stars add a bonus equal to the star count.
- **Reverse!** The beam changes direction after star 21, star 55, and once more late in the run.
- The beam speeds up with every star, and slows slightly on your last life.
- The run ends after 3 misses (**Game over**) or at 144 stars (**Galaxy complete!**).

## Modes
- **Daily galaxy:** the same run for everyone each day (speed, reversals, supernovas). Only your first run counts. It builds a day streak and gives a spoiler-free share result.
- **Endless:** a fresh random run, to practice and chase your high score.

## Feel
- Each star plays a note, and a perfect streak climbs the scale. A miss plays a thud.
- Haptics for each grade, a success pulse for supernovas and milestones.
- A ripple on each new star, a spark burst at milestones, a shake on a miss, and the screen dims on your last life.

## Look
Deep midnight blue with a faint starfield. Stars glow gold (perfect), pink (great) and cyan (close); misses leave a hollow ring. Rounded type (Fredoka).

## Later
- A share card that's a picture of your galaxy
- Galaxy types to unlock, each with a different spiral angle and palette
- Zen mode with no lives
- Game Center high scores

## Why it should pass App Review
It is a complete native game, not a website in a wrapper. It has scoring, progression, sound and haptics, and works fully offline. It needs no account and collects no data.
