## Description
A beam sweeps around a galaxy. Tap the moment it hits the ring, and a new star lands.

Hit your timing and the stars fall into a perfect spiral. Miss three times and it's game over. The beam speeds up with every star, changes direction when you least expect it, and gold supernova rings are worth 5×.

• One tap to play, and harder with every star
• Perfect streaks climb a musical scale
• A new daily galaxy every day, the same for everyone
• Day streaks and a shareable result
• No ads, no accounts, works offline
